Build a modern, responsive, mobile-first interactive digital product catalog website using HTML, Tailwind CSS, and standard JavaScript (or React). The website is designed as a digital directory for orthopedic surgeons, rehabilitation centers, and patients to explore advanced mobility solutions and submit patient referrals.

### 🎨 Design & Visual Guidelines:
- **Theme/Tone:** Clean, professional, modern medical/healthcare tech UI.
- **Color Palette:**
  - Primary: Deep Healthcare Blue (`#1E3A8A` or `#0F172A`)
  - Accent/Highlight: Modern Teal / Electric Cyan (`#06B6D4` or `#0D9488`)
  - Background: Clean Light Slate (`#F8FAFC`) with crisp white card containers (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Typography:** Sans-serif (Inter or System UI font stack), clean line-height, with full support for RTL Arabic text where applicable.
- **UI Elements:** Floating navigation bar, hover effects on product cards, modal windows for detailed specifications, interactive filtering, and sticky referral call-to-action buttons.

---

### 🗂️ Complete Site Map & Content Architecture:

#### 1. Home / Main Landing Hub (`/index.html` or `/`)
- **Hero Section:**
  - Catchy Headline: "Advanced Prosthetic & Orthotic Mobility Solutions"
  - Subheadline: "Interactive clinical directory for medical specialists, orthopedic surgeons, and physical therapy centers."
  - CTA Buttons: "Explore Catalog" (scrolls to products) and "Refer a Patient" (launches referral modal or navigates to form).
- **Quick Category Grid (Card Layout):**
  - 🦿 **Prosthetic Solutions** (Interactive socket systems, bionics, microprocessors)
  - 🦴 **Orthotic Bracing** (Spinal braces, AFOs/KAFOs, custom 3D insoles)
  - 📹 **Clinical Case Studies** (Patient outcomes & gait demonstrations)
  - 📋 **Doctor & Clinic Referral** (Fast digital referral intake)

#### 3. Product Specification Modal (Pop-up Component)
- When a user clicks "View Clinical Specs" on any product card, open an interactive modal with:
  - Product Title & High-Res Image Placeholder
  - Key Clinical Benefits & Indications
  - Technical Specifications (Materials, Weight Limit, Adjustment Mechanism)
  - Action Button: "Refer Patient for this Device" (Pre-fills referral form with selected product name).

---

#### 4. Clinical Outcomes & Case Studies Section
- **Video / Media Gallery Grid:**
  - Embeddable placeholders for gait analysis videos, socket adjustment demos (e.g., Click Medical RP-200 dial assembly), and patient mobility tests.
  - Quick filters for `[ Before/After Gait ]`, `[ Socket Adjustments ]`, `[ Patient Interviews ]`.

---

#### 5. Interactive Digital Patient Referral Form Component
Include an accessible form (or modal popup) with the following fields:
1. **Referring Physician / Center Name** (Text input)
2. **Contact Number / Email** (Tel/Email input)
3. **Patient Age / Location** (Text input)
4. **Clinical Category** (Dropdown: Lower Limb Prosthetics, Upper Limb Bionics, Orthotic Bracing, Diabetic Footwear, General Consultation)
5. **Specific Device Requested** (Optional text input / auto-populated from product modal)
6. **Clinical Notes / Upload File** (Textarea + file upload button for X-rays or prescription notes)
7. **Submit Button:** Triggers a success confirmation modal with direct WhatsApp redirection link.

#### 6. Footer & Contact
- Clinic branding, location map placeholder (Amman, Jordan), direct WhatsApp contact button, and QR code modal trigger for instant mobile sharing.

### 💻 Technical Instructions for Replit:
1. Create clean, modular files (`index.html`, `style.css` or Tailwind CDN, `app.js`).
2. Add JavaScript logic for:
   - Dynamic product category filtering without page reloads.
   - Opening/closing product detail modals.
   - Simple referral form submission validation with a success popup.
3. Make sure all cards and grids are fully responsive on mobile devices (375px width and above).
